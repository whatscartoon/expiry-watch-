Delete Your Account — Expiry Watch

If you would like to delete your Expiry Watch account and associated data, please send a deletion request using the details below.

How to request deletion

Send an email to maxvirwala@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in to the app, with the subject line: "Delete My Expiry Watch Account".

Contact: maxvirwala@gmail.com

What gets deleted

  • Your email address (used for sign-in)
  • All product/inventory records associated with your account

Timeline

Your account and associated data will be deleted within 7 business days of receiving your request.
